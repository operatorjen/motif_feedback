OBSERVER-SPECIFIC MOTIFS

Provisional patterns each agent notices in this project. They do not alter persona memory. Recurrence is counted across distinct turns; aliases stay attached to one observer-owned thread. You decide how agents treat established pattern checkpoints.

PATTERN CHECKPOINTS

When an established sequence returns across at least three turns, you can let its observer simply notice it, keep following it, test its limits, or pause it. A checkpoint never changes memory, persona, motif status, or speaking order.

RECURRING SEQUENCE PATTERNS

These are actual repeated transitions, short sequences, and recursive returns supported across at least three distinct turns.

TRAJECTORY SUMMARY

Recurrence and transition variety summarize each observer's recorded path. Established motifs are preferred; raw observations remain diagnostic.
